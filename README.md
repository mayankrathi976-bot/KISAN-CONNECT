# KISAN-CONNECT
Kisan Connect: Real-time Market Prediction for Agricultural Commodities Project Overview

Kisan Connect empowers farmers and agricultural professionals to make informed decisions by providing real-time price prediction for various crops. We leverage cutting-edge AI technology, including Gemma 2.2B, to unlock insights from historical market data and generate accurate predictions.

Features:

Real-Time Price Prediction: Our innovative AI model predicts crop prices based on current market conditions and historical trends. Customization: Tailor predictions to your specific crops and regions. User-Friendly Interface: A simple and intuitive interface provides quick and easy access to real-time predictions. Historical Data Integration: Access historical market data to build a comprehensive understanding of price trends. Data Transparency: Gain insight into model predictions and understand the factors driving its output. Technical Details:

AI Model: We utilize the Gemma 2.2B model for price prediction. This powerful language model, trained on vast datasets of market data, is highly accurate and adaptable. Data Source: We leverage a carefully curated dataset of historical market data from reputable sources to train our model. This data includes historical price data, weather patterns, market volatility, and other relevant factors. Real-Time Updates: Our model is constantly updated with the latest market data to ensure the accuracy of our predictions. How it works:

User input: Enter the desired crop and region. Model prediction: The model utilizes its deep learning capabilities to predict the price range for the chosen crop. Visualization: A clear, visual representation of the prediction is provided for easy interpretation. Getting Started:

Sign up: Create an account to access our platform. Explore data: Explore our platform's historical data to understand market trends. Make predictions: Start making informed decisions about your agricultural ventures. Contributing to the project:
